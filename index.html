Privacy Policy - Tractor Khata

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tractor Khata ("we", "our", or "the app"), developed by Surojit Mondal, collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

We collect information solely to provide, operate, and maintain core application features:

  • Account Information (Email & Profile): When you sign in (via Google Sign-In or email authentication), we collect your email address and profile name/photo to manage your secure account and identity.
  • Ledger & Business Data: Information entered into the ledger (customer entries, records, notes, notebooks, and expenses) is stored securely to calculate balances, generate reports, and keep your business records synchronized.
  • Photos & Media: The app only accesses device photos or storage when you voluntarily choose to upload a profile image or a UPI payment QR code for transaction sharing. We do not access, scan, or read any other private photos or files.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

To support app maintenance and development, Tractor Khata displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. These third-party services may automatically collect certain device information, such as:

  • Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID)
  • Device identifiers, IP address, and network information
  • App usage and diagnostic data for ad performance and fraud prevention

These third-party providers handle your data in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services & Google AdMob Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase Privacy Policy

3. Data Storage & Security

All cloud synchronization is securely handled via Google Cloud Firestore and Firebase backend infrastructure. Data transmitted between your device and Firebase servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS protocols.

4. Account and Data Deletion

You have full ownership of your data and can request complete account and data deletion at any time.

How to request deletion:

  • Send an email to: mrhacker9064@gmail.com
  • Subject: "Account Deletion Request - Tractor Khata"
  • Specify your registered email address associated with the account.

Upon verification, all your personal information, ledger records, notes, expenses, and uploaded images will be permanently erased from our Firebase servers within 7 business days.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app improvements or regulatory updates. We encourage you to review this page periodically for any changes.

Privacy Policy - Tractor Khata

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tractor Khata ("we", "our", or "the app"), developed by Surojit Mondal, handles and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

1. User Authentication & Account Access

Tractor Khata supports multiple usage modes:

  • Google Sign-In & Email Authentication: When you choose to authenticate via Google Sign-In or email, we collect your email address and profile identity solely to maintain your account and cloud backup records.
  • Offline Mode: Users can choose "Skip for Now" to use the ledger locally on their device without creating an account or transmitting personal login data to our servers.
  • Third-Party Interface Notice (Facebook Option): Any user interface options or buttons indicating third-party integrations (such as Facebook login/link) that are inactive, unlinked, or under development do not collect, transmit, store, or process any personal user data.

2. App Features & Data Processing

  • Ledger, Notes & Business Expenses: Records, transaction entries, notebooks, notes, and diesel/maintenance costs entered into the app are stored securely to calculate balances, generate reports, and keep your records synced.
  • Photos & Media Permissions: The app accesses device storage/photos solely when you choose to upload a profile picture or a UPI QR code for receiving payments and sharing receipts. The app does not access, scan, or read any other private device files or images.
  • Customer Reminders & Messages: When you tap reminder options (Call, SMS, or WhatsApp), the app merely redirects to your device's external dialer or messaging applications with the recipient number or pre-filled reminder text. Tractor Khata does not access your personal call logs, read your private SMS, or monitor your external chat conversations.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Tractor Khata displays ads provided by Google AdMob. These services may automatically collect standard device information such as:

  • Google Advertising ID
  • Device identifiers, IP address, and network connection diagnostics
  • App usage data for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention

Data collected by Google services is governed by their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services & Google AdMob Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase Privacy Policy

4. Cloud Storage & Data Security

Cloud synchronization is managed via Google Cloud Firestore and Firebase backend infrastructure. All data transmitted between your device and Firebase servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS protocols.

5. Account and Data Deletion

You have full ownership of your data and can request complete account and data deletion at any time.

How to request deletion:

  • Send an email to: mrhacker9064@gmail.com
  • Subject: "Account Deletion Request - Tractor Khata"
  • Specify your registered email address associated with the account.

Upon verification, all personal info, cloud ledger records, notes, expenses, and uploaded images will be permanently erased from our Firebase servers within 7 business days.
